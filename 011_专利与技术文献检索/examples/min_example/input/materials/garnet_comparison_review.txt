Comparative Review: Interfacial Resistance in Garnet Solid Electrolytes
(SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE REVIEW - not a real paper)

Abstract: We compare interfacial resistance of garnet-type solid electrolytes across
reporting groups and discuss lithium dendrite suppression mechanisms in solid-state
batteries. A solid electrolyte with sub-50 ohm cm2 interfacial resistance remains rare.

Section 1: Measurement conventions. Interfacial resistance values for the garnet-type
solid electrolyte vary with stack pressure and polishing; we normalize before comparison.

Section 2: Dendrite suppression. Lithium dendrite growth in solid-state batteries
correlates with grain boundary chemistry of the oxide electrolyte.
